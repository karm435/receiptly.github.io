Gmail receipt import

Turn purchase emails into receipts.

On iPhone and iPad, connect Gmail in Integrations and browse your recent purchases. Choose the ones you want, review the merchant, date, amount, and line items, then save each one as a receipt. You can also use a PDF or image attachment as the receipt.

  • Read-only access. Receiptly never changes or deletes your emails.
  • Email text and attachments are processed on your device, never sent to cloud AI.
  • Disconnect any time. Receipts you saved stay in your library.

Gmail import is included with Receiptly Pro on iPhone and iPad. It is not part of the Android app. How Gmail import works.
