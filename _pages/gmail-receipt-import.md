## Why import receipts from email

Online orders, subscriptions, and delivery apps send receipts by email instead of paper. Gmail import brings those purchases into the same library as the receipts you scan, so your reports, exports, and warranty records cover everything you buy.

## What stays private

- Email text and attachments are processed on your iPhone or iPad. Gmail content is not sent to cloud AI services, even when Smart Scan is on for other receipts.
- Receiptly connects directly to Google. There is no Receiptly server that stores or reads your mailbox.
- Emails are held in memory only while you browse. The original email and attachment are not saved with the receipt.
- The receipt details you save, plus a marker that recognizes emails you already imported, become part of your library and can sync with iCloud.

Read the [Gmail section of the privacy policy](/privacypolicy/#gmail) for the full details.

## What you need

- Receiptly on iPhone or iPad with Receiptly Pro
- A Gmail account

Gmail import is not part of the Android app.
