About Receiptly

Karma Academy owns 'Receiptly', an app which helps users categorize and search for receipts, invoices, sales slips, gift cards, and vouchers and keep a record of their shopping history. The app uses optical character recognition (OCR) to convert images into text.

Receiptly is designed for in-store shopping and saving the hassle of finding receipts. In addition, it gives users freedom from physical copies while maintaining user data privacy. With powerful features like warranty tracking, smart tagging, and iCloud sync, Receiptly makes receipt management effortless.

Our latest updates include iPad optimization, multiple language support, and advanced batch processing capabilities, making Receiptly the most comprehensive receipt management solution on the App Store.

Our Mission
