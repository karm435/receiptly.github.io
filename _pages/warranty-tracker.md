## Why keep warranties with receipts

A warranty claim usually needs proof of purchase. Keeping the warranty date with the receipt puts both in one place when a fridge, laptop, or pair of headphones stops working.

## Built into your receipt library

- On iPhone and iPad, the Warranties screen separates expiring and expired items, with reminders from one day to one month before the end date.
- Apple Shortcuts can check upcoming warranties on iPhone.
- Products are searchable, so you can find a receipt by what you bought.
- Your receipts stay on your device, with optional iCloud sync across your Apple devices.
