Frequently asked questions

How do I search for a receipt?

You can use receipt, store, tag or product name to search for a receipt on the home screen.

Installed app on a new device and it is not showing my data?

Sometimes iCloud does not trigger the refresh. Navigate to Settings → Apple ID Account → iCloud. After that just come back to the app and you should see the data.

Do I lose all my images if I delete the app?

No, when you create receipt using camera, the images are also saved in your photo library. Also, the iCloud sync is ON for the app and your data is saved on iCloud.

How do I delete a receipt?

You can navigate to All receipts page and can swipe to delete a receipt.

How do I create/delete a store?

While creating a receipt, when you navigate to store page, you can create a new store or swipe to delete an existing store.

How do I contact for support?

You can reach us at support@receiptsco.app or post on Twitter @ReceiptsC and we will try to respond.
