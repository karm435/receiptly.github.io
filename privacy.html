Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority

Last updated: October 19, 2020

Privacy First

We do not collect any personal information. All your data stays on your device and in your iCloud (if enabled).

1.0 Information We Collect

We do not collect any information related to you. Receiptly is designed with privacy at its core, ensuring that all your receipt data remains completely private and under your control.

1.2 Information you provide to us

All the images and data you provide stays on your device. When you take photos of receipts or add information, this data is stored locally on your device and optionally synchronized through your personal iCloud account if you choose to enable iCloud sync.

2.0 What Personal Information we collect about you and how we collect it

We do not collect any personal information. Receiptly operates entirely on your device, processing receipt images and data locally without sending any information to external servers.

3.0 How we keep and secure the Personal Information we collect

All the data provided by you stays on your device and, if you choose to enable it, in your personal iCloud storage. If you wish to delete all the data, simply delete the app from your device. Your iCloud data can be managed through your iCloud settings.

The app uses Apple's built-in security features to protect your data, including device-level encryption and secure iCloud synchronization when enabled.

4.0 Data Processing

When you use the camera feature to capture receipts, the optical character recognition (OCR) processing happens entirely on your device. No images or extracted text are sent to external services or our servers.

5.0 Third-Party Services

Receiptly does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app operates independently without sharing data with external parties.

6.0 Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@receiptsco.app.
