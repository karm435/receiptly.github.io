## About Karma Academy {#karma-academy}

Karma Academy is an Australian-registered business based in Brisbane. We build apps for iPhone, iPad, and Android, and we also ship software for other Apple platforms including macOS, watchOS, and tvOS. We’re passionate about creating simple, elegant solutions that solve real-world problems.

## About Receiptly {#receiptly}

Karma Academy owns Receiptly, an app that helps you scan, categorize, and search receipts, invoices, sales slips, gift cards, and vouchers, and keep a record of your shopping history. The app uses optical character recognition (OCR) to turn images into text you can review.

Receiptly is designed for in-store shopping and saving the hassle of finding paper receipts. It gives you freedom from physical copies while keeping your library on the device. On iPhone and iPad, optional iCloud sync can share that library across your Apple devices. On Android, receipts stay on that phone and do not sync with iPhone.

On iPhone and iPad, you can also [connect Gmail and turn purchase emails into receipts](/gmail-receipt-import/), with email content processed on your device. [Warranty tracking](/warranty-tracker/), tags, reports, and [PDF, CSV, or Excel export](/receipt-export/) help after the purchase. Receiptly is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1532569828) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.karmafyapps.receiptly).
