## Which format to use

- **PDF** for a readable report to keep or send.
- **CSV** for importing into accounting or budgeting tools.
- **Excel** for sorting, totals, and your own formulas.

## Good for

- Tax time and end of financial year records
- Expense reimbursement at work
- Rental property and small business bookkeeping, with a category for each property or project

## Availability

Export is included with Receiptly Pro on iPhone and iPad, and with Receiptly Plus on Android.
