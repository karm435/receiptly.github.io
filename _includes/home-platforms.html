Receiptly for iOS and Android.

Scan receipts, organize purchases, track expenses and warranties, and export reports. Storage and integrations depend on your device.

iPhone & iPad

Keep your receipt library on your device, with optional iCloud sync across your Apple devices.

  • Scan with Apple’s on-device text recognition.
  • Use Apple Shortcuts to create expenses and find receipts.
  • Connect Gmail to turn purchase emails into receipts. Email content is processed on your device.

Android

Keep your receipt library on your Android device, with no Receiptly account to create.

  • Scan with on-device text recognition from Google ML Kit.
  • Choose optional Smart Scan for extra help with receipt images using Google’s cloud AI services.
  • The Android app does not include Gmail import or sync with iPhone, iPad, or iCloud.
Download for Android ↗ Android privacy details

Smart Scan is optional on both platforms and can send a reduced receipt image to Google’s cloud AI services. Gmail imports on iOS use a separate, on-device process. Read about scanning and data use.
