About Receiptly

Karma Academy owns Receiptly, an app that helps you scan, categorize, and search receipts, invoices, sales slips, gift cards, and vouchers, and keep a record of your shopping history. The app uses optical character recognition (OCR) to turn images into text you can review.

Receiptly is designed for in-store shopping and saving the hassle of finding paper receipts. It gives you freedom from physical copies while keeping your library on the device. On iPhone and iPad, optional iCloud sync can share that library across your Apple devices. On Android, receipts stay on that phone and do not sync with iPhone.

Warranty tracking, tags, reports, and PDF, CSV, or Excel export help after the purchase. Receiptly is on the App Store and Google Play.
